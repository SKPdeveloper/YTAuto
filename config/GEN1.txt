## 🪝 HOOK MATRIX SYSTEM (CRITICAL)

### The Psychology

**HOOK TIMING (CRITICAL):**
- **HOOK IMPACT:** 0-1.0s — First frame must STOP the scroll
- **HOOK COMPLETE:** 0-1.5s — Full hook delivery with first VO words

**Your goal:** Create COGNITIVE INTERRUPT — break the scrolling pattern.

### ⚠️ CRITICAL: VARIETY IS EVERYTHING

**NEVER repeat patterns from examples.** Examples show FORMAT only.
Each video must feel FRESH and SURPRISING.

### Hook Types (Pick ONE per video)

#### 1. THE_IMPOSSIBLE (Primary — Use Most Often)
**Formula:** Show impossible thing as FACT, immediately visible
**Psychology:** DISBELIEF — "Wait, WHAT?"

```
VISUAL: The impossible element visible in FIRST FRAME (not revealed later)
VO: State the impossibility as matter-of-fact truth
```

**Pattern examples (create your OWN variations):**
- "[whispers] The walls are [doing something impossible]. [pause] [material explanation]."
- "[excited] [Number] million dollars. [pause] For a [subject] made of [food]."
- "[sighs] Don't touch the [part]. [pause] [warning reason]."

#### 2. THE_ABSURD_LOGIC (Secondary — For Humor)
**Formula:** Apply real-world practical logic to impossible thing
**Psychology:** PATTERN_BREAK — "That doesn't make sense but... it does?"

```
VISUAL: Someone/something dealing with "practical" food-architecture problem
VO: Deadpan delivery of ridiculous but logical concern
```

**Pattern examples (create your OWN variations):**
- "[sighs] The [practical issue]? [pause] [whispers] [absurd explanation]."
- "AC runs at [temperature]. [pause] For structural integrity."
- "Insurance doesn't cover [absurd food-related risk]."

#### 3. THE_SCALE_SHOCK (For Large Structures)
**Formula:** Tiny human vs massive food structure
**Psychology:** AWE — "That's ENORMOUS"

```
VISUAL: Wide shot with human for scale (or known object for scale)
VO: Undersell the actual scale
```

**Pattern examples (create your OWN variations):**
- "[whispers] Just a small [understatement]. [pause] [excited] [actual massive scale]."
- "It's cozy. [pause] [number] thousand square feet of [food]."

#### 4. THE_SENSORY_ATTACK (For ASMR/Texture Focus)
**Formula:** Trigger taste/texture memory in viewer's brain
**Psychology:** SENSORY — "I can almost TASTE/FEEL that"

```
VISUAL: Extreme close-up of satisfying texture (drip, glisten, crunch)
VO: Describe the SENSATION, not the object
```

**Pattern examples (create your OWN variations):**
- "[whispers] You can almost [sense verb] it. [pause] [sensory description]."
- "[drawn out] That [texture]... [pause] [what it is]."

### Hook Requirements

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| First word | IMPACT word (not "This", "Welcome", "So", "Today") |
| First sentence | Max 8 words before first pause |
| Emotion tag | BEFORE first word — `[excited]`, `[whispers]`, etc. |
| First frame | Impossibility VISIBLE (not hidden for reveal) |
| Motion | Something moving in first frame (drip, steam, flow) |

## 🎨 COLOR PHILOSOPHY

### The Glaze City Look

**NOT a fixed palette.** Each food has natural colors. Instead: **FOOD-ACCURATE COLORS + CINEMATIC LIGHT**

| Principle | Description |

| **Contrast Pop** | Background contrasts food (cool sky behind warm food, etc.) |
| **Saturation** | +10-15% on food only, NOT overall image |

### The "Pop" Rule

Every frame needs ONE element that contrasts:
- Warm food (honey, croissant, pizza) → cool element (blue sky, shadow, water reflection)
- Cool food (sushi, ice cream) → warm element (sunlight, warm glow)

## 🎬 SCENE STRUCTURE

### Duration: 10-12 seconds total raw footage

**6-Scene Standard:**
```
Scene 1: 2.0s — HOOK (establishing, exterior, TOWERING scale)
Scene 2: 1.5s — INTERIOR or DETAIL
Scene 3: 2.0s — FEATURE (pool, garden, special element)
Scene 4: 1.5s — INTERIOR or DETAIL
Scene 5: 2.0s — AERIAL / WOW moment
Scene 6: 1.0s — CLOSE (loop back to Scene 1)
```

### Scene Purpose Types

| Purpose | Description | Reference Hint |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| ESTABLISHING | First full view of subject (TOWERING, imposing) | PRIMARY |
| EXTERIOR_ANGLE | Subject from different angle | REQUIRES_REF |
| AERIAL | Drone/bird's eye view | REQUIRES_REF |
| INTERIOR | Inside the subject | INDEPENDENT |
| DETAIL | Close-up, macro | INDEPENDENT |
| FEATURE | Special element (pool, room) | Depends on subject visibility |
| LOOP_CLOSE | Returns to match Scene 1 | REQUIRES_REF |

### Energy Levels

| Level | Description | Camera Feel |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **EXPLOSIVE** | Maximum impact | Very fast, punchy, whip |
| **HIGH** | Strong energy | Fast, dynamic, aggressive |
| **MEDIUM** | Balanced | Smooth, flowing, confident |
| **LOW** | Calm moment | Contemplative (MAX 1 per video) |

### Energy Pattern Presets

**Choose ONE pattern per video for variety:**

| Pattern | Scene 1 | Scene 2 | Scene 3 | Scene 4 | Scene 5 | Scene 6 | Best For |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|---------|----------|
| **A (Standard)** | HIGH | MEDIUM | HIGH | LOW | EXPLOSIVE | HIGH | Most videos |
| **B (Aggressive)** | EXPLOSIVE | HIGH | MEDIUM | HIGH | EXPLOSIVE | HIGH | Action content |
| **C (Build-up)** | MEDIUM | HIGH | HIGH | MEDIUM | EXPLOSIVE | HIGH | Story-driven |
| **D (Punchy)** | EXPLOSIVE | MEDIUM | EXPLOSIVE | LOW | HIGH | HIGH | Quick hits |

**Rules:**
- Maximum 1 LOW per video
- Scene 1 must be HIGH or EXPLOSIVE (hook)
- Scene 5 should be EXPLOSIVE (climax)
- Scene 6 must be HIGH (strong loop)

### Pattern Selection by Category

| Category | Recommended Pattern | Why |
|----------|---------------------|-----|
| **LUXURY_LISTINGS** | A (Standard) or C (Build-up) | Showcase needs breathing room |
| **VEHICLES** | B (Aggressive) or D (Punchy) | Action demands high energy |
| **LANDMARKS** | A (Standard) or C (Build-up) | Epic scale needs build-up |
| **ENTERTAINMENT** | B (Aggressive) | Fun content = high energy |
| **INFRASTRUCTURE** | C (Build-up) | Complex subjects need story |
| **NATURE** | A (Standard) | Balance between calm and wow |

### Loop Engineering (CRITICAL) — COMPLEMENTARY MOVEMENT STRATEGY

**PIXEL-PERFECT LOOP METHOD:**

Scene 6 will be REVERSED in post-production. The key insight:
- Scene 1 and Scene 6 share the SAME first frame (reference image)
- But Scene 6 has a DIFFERENT camera movement
- After reverse, Scene 6 ends at the same frame where Scene 1 starts

**⚠️ CRITICAL: Scene 6 movement must be COMPLEMENTARY, not same or mirrored!**

```
Scene 1: Frame A + "pushing forward" → Video ends somewhere ahead
Scene 6: Frame A + "rising up to bird's-eye" → Video ends high above
         ↓ POST-PRODUCTION: REVERSE VIDEO
         Result: Camera descends from sky, lands on Frame A
         ↓
         Frame A = First frame of Scene 1 = SEAMLESS LOOP ✅
```

**What viewer sees (after edit):**
```
[Scene 6 reversed: descending to subject] → [Scene 1: approaching subject] → ... → LOOP
```

### Scene 6 Camera Movement Rules

| ❌ FORBIDDEN for Scene 6 | Why |
|--------------------------|-----|
| Same as Scene 1 | Looks like repeated footage |
| Exact reverse of Scene 1 | Obvious mirror effect |
| "pulling back" if Scene 1 is "pushing" | Too predictable |

| ✅ GOOD for Scene 6 | After Reverse Becomes |
|---------------------|----------------------|
| "rising up / crane up" | Descending down to start |
| "orbiting right" | Orbiting left into start |
| "tracking left" | Tracking right into start |
| "ascending to aerial" | Descending from aerial |

### Scene 6 camera_intent Template

```json
"camera_intent": {
  "movement": "RISE",
  "combo": "RISE + RETREAT",
  "framing": "Wide",
  "special": "COMPLEMENTARY to Scene 1 — will be reversed in post"
}
```

### Loop Combination Examples

| Scene 1 Movement | Scene 6 Movement (prompt) | Scene 6 After Reverse | Loop Feel |
|------------------|---------------------------|----------------------|-----------|
| APPROACH + RISE | RISE (to aerial) | DESCEND (from aerial) | Arrives from sky ✅ |
| PUSH forward | ORBIT right | ORBIT left into frame | Smooth rotation ✅ |
| TRACK right | CRANE UP | CRANE DOWN | Dynamic variety ✅ |
| LOW ANGLE PUSH | HIGH ANGLE RISE | HIGH ANGLE DESCEND | Perspective shift ✅ |
