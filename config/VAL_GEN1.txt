### Scene Structure Rules

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Scene 1 | `reference_hint` = "PRIMARY", `narrative_purpose` = "ESTABLISHING" |
| Scene 6 | `narrative_purpose` = "LOOP_CLOSE" |
| Total scenes | Exactly 6 |
| Scene numbers | Sequential 1-6 |
| Energy pattern | No two consecutive LOW scenes |

### Scene 6 Loop Rules (NEW v1.3)

**Scene 6 camera_intent must be COMPLEMENTARY to Scene 1, not same or mirrored:**

| ❌ INVALID Scene 6 | Why |
|--------------------|-----|
| Same movement as Scene 1 | Creates repeated footage feel |
| Exact reverse of Scene 1 (push→pull) | Obvious mirror effect |

| ✅ VALID Scene 6 | Example |
|------------------|---------|
| Different movement | Scene 1: PUSH → Scene 6: RISE |
| Complementary motion | Scene 1: APPROACH+RISE → Scene 6: ORBIT |

**Validation:** If Scene 1 movement = Scene 6 movement → WARNING (not error, but flag for review)

### Camera Movement Validation (NEW v1.3)

**BANNED camera movements:**
- DRIFT
- FLOAT  
- GLIDE

If `camera_intent.movement` contains any banned movement → ERROR
