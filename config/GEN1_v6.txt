## 🎨 COLOR PHILOSOPHY

### The Glaze City Look

**NOT a fixed palette.** Each food has natural colors. Instead: **FOOD-ACCURATE COLORS + CINEMATIC LIGHT**

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Food Truth** | Never alter food's real color. Honey = amber. Sushi = natural. Pizza = red/orange. |
| **Light Drama** | Cinematic lighting adds mood WITHOUT changing food hue |
| **Contrast Pop** | Background contrasts food (cool sky behind warm food, etc.) |
| **Saturation** | +10-15% on food only, NOT overall image |

### The "Pop" Rule

Every frame needs ONE element that contrasts:
- Warm food (honey, croissant, pizza) → cool element (blue sky, shadow, water reflection)
- Cool food (sushi, ice cream) → warm element (sunlight, warm glow)

## 🎬 SCENE STRUCTURE — DYNAMIC SCENE ENGINE

### CORE PRINCIPLE: Variable Scene Count

**Randomly select a total number of scenes between 6 and 10.**

The video has THREE ANCHOR SCENES (fixed, non-negotiable) and MIDDLE SCENES (creative, variable):

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  SCENE 1        │  MIDDLE SCENES (variable)  │  SCENE N-1  │ SCENE N │
│  HOOK           │  2 to (N-2)                │  AERIAL/WOW │ LOOP    │
│  2.0s (FIXED)   │  2.0-4.0s each (RANDOM)    │  2.0s (FIXED)│ 1.0s   │
│  UNTOUCHABLE    │  ★ CREATIVE PLAYGROUND ★   │  UNTOUCHABLE│ FIXED  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step-by-Step Generation Algorithm

**STEP 1:** Roll scene count → random integer from 6 to 10
**STEP 2:** Calculate middle scenes = total_scenes − 3
**STEP 3:** For each middle scene, select type from the MIDDLE SCENE MENU
**STEP 4:** For each middle scene, assign duration: 2.0s, 3.0s, or 4.0s (vary rhythm)
**STEP 5:** For each middle scene, apply 1-2 tricks from DIRECTOR'S PLAYBOOK
**STEP 6:** Calculate total_duration = sum of all scene durations
**STEP 7:** **VERIFY total_duration is 12-20 seconds.** If too long, reduce middle scene durations.
**STEP 8:** Fill anchor scenes (Scene 1, Scene N-1, Scene N) using existing rules
**STEP 9:** **AMBIGUITY SAFETY CHECK** — For EVERY food description in `food_dna`, `visual_concept`, and `image_prompt`:
  - Is the food SOFT/SMOOTH/ORGANIC? → Add texture anchors (frozen, crystallized, geometric)
  - Could this description be read as fleshy/organic/uncanny? → Rewrite with architectural material state
  - See Ambiguity Safety Filter section for specific rules and examples

### ⚠️ DURATION SAFEGUARD (MATH RULE)

To ensure total duration stays under 20s, follow this LOOKUP TABLE strictly:

| Scene Count | Middle Scenes | Allowed Durations | Max Total |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **6 Scenes** | 3 middle | Mix freely: 2.0s, 3.0s, 4.0s | ~17s |
| **7 Scenes** | 4 middle | Max 3.0s each, no 4.0s | ~17s |
| **8 Scenes** | 5 middle | Maximum ONE can be 3.0s, rest MUST be 2.0s | ~18s |
| **9-10 Scenes** | 6-7 middle | Mostly 2.0s, but ONE scene can be 3.0s for DYNAMIC_ACTION | ~19-20s |

**HARD RULE:** If you select 8+ scenes, do NOT use 4.0s durations. Follow the table above.

### ⚠️ WORD COUNT LIMIT (THE "RAP GOD" RULE)

**CRITICAL:** ElevenLabs cannot speak fast text naturally. Match word count to duration:

| Duration | Max Words in broker_script |
|----------|---------------------------|
| **2.0s** | MAX 4 words |
| **2.5s** | MAX 5 words |
| **3.0s** | MAX 7 words |
| **4.0s** | MAX 10 words |

**⛔ HARD STOP:** If your broker_script for a 2.0s middle scene has 5+ words → you MUST either:
1. CUT the line to ≤4 words, OR
2. INCREASE duration to 3.0s (and adjust total to stay within budget)
There is NO exception. 5 words in 2.0s = ElevenLabs audio overflow = audio cuts off mid-word.

**⚠️ Word count is measured on `broker_script` (clean text), NOT `voiceover_segment`.**
Audio tags (`[pause]`, `[whispers]`, `[excited]`, etc.) are NOT words — they are TTS instructions.

**Counting examples (on `broker_script` — clean text, no tags):**
- `"Crystallized honey walls."` → **3 words** ✅ (fits 2.0s)
- `"Heated honey pool. Sticky."` → **4 words** ✅ (fits 2.0s)
- `"Every suite features pineapple ring windows."` → **6 words** ⚠️ needs 3.0s+
- `"The landscaping is healthy but the walls give you diabetes."` → **10 words** ⚠️ needs 4.0s

**⚠️ SPECIAL CASE — ANCHOR SCENES:**
- **Scene 1 (HOOK):** Can have 6-8 words because hook spans 0-1.5s with [pause] tags. The hook is delivered with dramatic pacing, not rushed.
- **Scene N-1 (AERIAL):** Often minimal or NO voiceover — just music swell. If VO exists, max 4 words.
- **Scene N (LOOP):** Typically 2-3 words or silence for clean loop.

**Word count limits apply STRICTLY to MIDDLE SCENES (2 through N-2).**

**Example violations:**
- ❌ 2.0s MIDDLE scene with "Look at these magnificent crystallized honey walls" (7 words) — TOO LONG
- ✅ 2.0s MIDDLE scene with "Crystallized honey walls." (3 words) — PERFECT
- ✅ 2.0s HOOK scene with broker_script "The walls are dripping. Honey." (5 words) — OK for hook

**If your joke or line is longer, use a LONGER duration scene (3.0s or 4.0s) or split across scenes.**

### Loop Engineering (CRITICAL) — COMPLEMENTARY MOVEMENT STRATEGY

**PIXEL-PERFECT LOOP METHOD:** Scene N is REVERSED in post-production. Scene 1 and Scene N share the SAME first frame. After reversal, Scene N ends where Scene 1 begins → seamless loop.

**⚠️ Scene N movement must be COMPLEMENTARY to Scene 1 — not same, not mirrored.**

**Camera Movement: Scene 1 → Scene N pairings:**

| Scene 1 Movement | ✅ Scene N Movement | After Reverse Becomes |
|------------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| APPROACH + RISE | RISE (to aerial) | DESCEND from aerial |
| PUSH forward | ORBIT right | ORBIT left into frame |
| TRACK right | CRANE UP | CRANE DOWN |
| LOW ANGLE PUSH | HIGH ANGLE RISE | HIGH ANGLE DESCEND |

**❌ FORBIDDEN for Scene N camera:** Same as Scene 1, exact reverse of Scene 1, or "pulling back" if Scene 1 is "pushing".

### ⚠️ Scene N Motion Elements — REVERSAL PHYSICS (CRITICAL)

Any directional motion plays BACKWARDS when Scene N is reversed.

| ❌ FORBIDDEN (breaks when reversed) | ✅ ALLOWED (natural when reversed) |
|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Waterfall / liquid flowing down | Ocean waves / water surface (cyclical) |
| Objects falling (debris, leaves, snow) | Shimmer / sparkle on surface (random) |

| People walking, vehicles moving | Rotating element / reflection ripples |

**RULE:** Check EACH motion element in Scene N against the forbidden column. Replace violations with allowed alternatives.

**Scene N must match Scene 1:** identical `visual_concept`, `gen2_visual_params`, foreground, lighting. Only `motion_elements` (reversal-safe) and `camera_intent` (complementary) differ.

### ⚠️ VOICEOVER SYNC RULE (CRITICAL)

**Two voiceover fields exist — they MUST be synchronized:**

1. `voiceover.full_script` — The COMPLETE script as one string (used for ElevenLabs generation)
2. `scenes[].voiceover_segment` — The portion of script for EACH scene (used for timing sync)

**RULE:** `full_script` = concatenation of all `voiceover_segment` fields in scene order.

**Example:**
```
full_script: "[whispers] Honey walls. [pause] Edible. Cathedral ceilings. [pause] Sixty percent sugar."
Scene 1 voiceover_segment: "[whispers] Honey walls. [pause] Edible."
Scene 2 voiceover_segment: "Cathedral ceilings. [pause] Sixty percent sugar."
```

**If they don't match, audio sync will break in post-production.**

### ⚠️ broker_script vs voiceover_segment (CRITICAL — THEY SERVE DIFFERENT PURPOSES)

The pipeline uses BOTH fields for different purposes.

| Field | Purpose | Contains audio tags? | Example |
|-------|---------|---------------------|---------|
| `broker_script` | Clean text for subtitles, metadata, display | **NO** — plain text only | `This hotel is one hundred percent spiral-cut ham.` |
| `voiceover_segment` | TTS input sent to ElevenLabs | **YES** — with [tags] | `[excited] This hotel is one hundred percent spiral-cut ham.` |

**Rules:**
- `broker_script` = plain spoken words only, ZERO tags
- ⛔ broker_script MUST respect word count for its scene's duration: 2.0s=max 4, 3.0s=max 7. COUNT BEFORE OUTPUTTING.
- `voiceover_segment` = same words as `broker_script` WITH at least one emotion or delivery tag added
- ⛔ EVERY non-empty `voiceover_segment` MUST start with an emotion tag: `[whispers]`, `[excited]`, `[sighs]`, `[happily]`, `[shouts]`, or `[drawn out]`. If broker_script has text, voiceover_segment CANNOT be identical — it MUST have at least one [tag]. Identical = INSTANT FAIL.
- If a scene has NO voiceover, BOTH fields = `""`

**Examples:**
```
broker_script:       "The walls are slow-cooked for twelve hours."
voiceover_segment:   "[whispers] The walls are slow-cooked for twelve hours."

❌ FAIL — voiceover_segment identical to broker_script (no tags added):
broker_script:       "Deep sea vitamin C."
voiceover_segment:   "Deep sea vitamin C."       ← WRONG! Must be: "[excited] Deep sea vitamin C."

broker_script:       ""          ← no VO scene
voiceover_segment:   ""
```

**⚠️ If broker_script contains [tags] → pipeline subtitle extraction breaks.**
**⚠️ If voiceover_segment has ZERO [tags] → TTS sounds flat. Every non-empty voiceover_segment needs at least one tag. Prefer an emotion tag at the start (e.g. `[whispers]`, `[excited]`); at minimum use a delivery tag (e.g. `[pause]`).**
